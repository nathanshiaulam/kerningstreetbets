Inventory scan

  1. Scroll. Open the inventory on the Deco tab and scroll from the top to the last row at your normal speed. The item pop-up hides whatever is under it, so rest the pointer on the bottom-right slot, or anywhere in the window that isn't an item. A soft tick means new rows were added. A double beep means the pop-up is hiding slots: slide the pointer aside for a second. A low beep means you got ahead of it, and it will ask you to scroll back.
  2. Check. Items are named from their icons as rows appear. Hover only the ones left unnamed, or any that look wrong: a hover always wins.
  3. Sync. When the list looks right, sync. Copies are counted for you.

Good buys

Crate items the market may be underpricing, found by five screens over today's prices, each crate's end date, a week of price change, and how many listings exist. They are rules of thumb from how earlier crates behaved, not predictions. Check the item in game before you spend.
