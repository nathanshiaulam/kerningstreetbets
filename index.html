Inventory scan

  1. Map it. Open the inventory on the Deco tab. Keep the pointer off the items (the title bar or scrollbar works) and scroll from the top to the last row, a notch at a time. A soft tick means new rows were added.
  2. Name it. Hover each different item once. A rising two-tone beep means a new name was read. A low beep means it was already known.
  3. Sync it. Come back here, check the list, and sync. Copies are counted from the map, so one hover per kind of item is enough, and names you have already synced are remembered next time.
